# Mosswharf Island

## Play online

[Play Mosswharf](https://intrepion.github.io/kata-animal-crossing-clone-gpt-6-luna-extra-high/) — free, playable in your browser.

A browser-playable island life sim inspired by the cozy routines of Animal Crossing: New Horizons. Walk around a continuous 3/4-view island, meet its residents, gather and catch things, make tools, decorate outdoors, visit the shop and museum, and expand a home through staged loans. Mosswharf, its residents, and its artwork are original.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. There is no build step or dependency install.

| Action | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Walk | **WASD** or **arrow keys**; click a place to walk there |
| Talk, pick up, or shake a tree | **E** |
| Use the equipped tool | **Space** |
| Equip net, fishing rod, axe, or shovel | **1–4** or click the tool belt |
| Open NookPhone | **Tab** |
| Open pockets | **B** |
| Show/hide island map | **M** |
| Close a panel or conversation | **Escape** |

## Island activities

- Shake fruit and palm trees, collect dropped fruit, branches, shells, weeds, and flowers.
- Catch a moving butterfly with the net. Cast from the river, pond, or shore, then press **Space** when the bobber gets a bite.
- Use the axe on trees and the shovel on fossils and rocks to gather crafting materials.
- Craft replacement tools from the NookPhone's DIY Recipes app.
- Craft and place a campfire from your pockets to decorate the island.
- Sell finds at Nook's Cranny, donate fossils to Blathers, pay Bells toward your home loan, and build room additions.
- Your inventory, tools, discoveries, furniture, home level, loan balance, and position save in this browser.

The resident illustrations are bundled in `assets/island-residents.png`; all other scene art is drawn by the game canvas. The game runs locally without external services or dependencies.
