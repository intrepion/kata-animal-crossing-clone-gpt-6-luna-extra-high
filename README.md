# Mosswharf Island

A small, original cozy island game inspired by the gentle routines of life-sim games. Explore the shore, shake fruit from trees, gather beach finds, catch a fish, and sell your haul to help the island grow.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. There is no build step or dependency install.

- Move with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**. Click a spot on the island to walk there.
- Press **E** or use the **Pick up** button near an island find.
- Press **F** to fish when you are near the water.
- Sell your pocket finds from the bag panel. Progress saves in this browser.

The original game is a compact, single-screen experience rather than a complete commercial-game recreation. All island art and characters are drawn in the page.
